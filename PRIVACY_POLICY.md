# Privacy Policy for VibeContent Pro

**Effective Date:** May 22, 2026

VibeContent Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension handles your data.

## 1. Information We Collect

VibeContent Pro operates locally on your device. We **do not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or user data to our servers.

The extension requires certain permissions to function, which are used strictly locally:
* **Webpage Content (activeTab / host permissions):** We read the text content of the webpage you are currently viewing solely for the purpose of generating content summaries, articles, or social media posts. This data is processed in real-time and is never stored or logged by us.
* **API Keys:** We use a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) model. Your OpenAI (or compatible) API key is saved locally in your browser's secure `chrome.storage.local`. It is only transmitted directly from your browser to the AI provider (e.g., OpenAI) to process your requests. We never have access to your API key.
* **Saved Content (Vault):** Any generated content you choose to save is stored entirely on your local machine using Chrome's local storage. We do not have access to your vault.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

The data accessed by the extension is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of VibeContent Pro:
* Reading the webpage context to generate accurate and relevant content.
* Communicating directly with your provided AI service via your API key.

We do not use your data for analytics, advertising, or any other unauthorized purposes. 

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties. Your data is only transmitted to the third-party AI provider (e.g., OpenAI) that you configure via your API key, and this transmission happens directly from your browser. Please review the privacy policy of your chosen AI provider to understand how they handle your prompts.

## 4. User Rights and Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can delete your saved vault content and your API key at any time by:
1. Uninstalling the VibeContent Pro extension.
2. Or, clearing the extension's local storage via the extension's Settings panel or your browser's extension management page.

## 5. Compliance with Chrome Web Store Policies

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the support link provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
