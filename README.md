# VibeContent Pro 🚀

**VibeContent Pro** is an AI-powered Chrome Extension designed to extract content from any webpage and instantly transform it into highly engaging, humanized social media posts.

## 🌟 Features

- **BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)**: Total privacy and control. Use your own API keys for Groq, Google Gemini, or xAI.
- **Dynamic Content Extraction**: Seamlessly extracts the main content from any active tab or allows manual text pasting.
- **Advanced Vibe Controls**: Dial in the exact `Humanization`, `Tone`, and `Length` for your posts.
- **Custom Prompts Library**: Save your favorite prompts (like "Twitter Post" or "LinkedIn Deep Dive") and switch between them instantly.
- **Anti-AI Fingerprinting**: Analyzes and estimates the "Human Score" of the generated text to ensure it bypasses AI detectors and feels completely natural.
- **Local Content Vault**: Save your generated posts directly inside the extension for later use. Everything is stored locally in your browser.

## 🔒 Privacy First
VibeContent Pro respects your data. We operate on a strict **local-first** architecture.
- Your API keys are encrypted and stored in your browser's local storage.
- The extension does not track you, does not use analytics, and never transmits your API keys or generated content to our servers.
- Read our full [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## 🚀 Installation (Developer Mode)

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the folder containing this extension.
5. Pin the extension to your toolbar and open its settings to enter your preferred API key!
